Our Story

Roofing is a lifelong craft in our family

Rocco's Roofing, a certified roofing contractor for GAF, is a family-owned business that has been providing high-quality roofing services to commercial and residential properties throughout New England since 2005.

Owner Joe Bianculli has nearly 40 years in the roofing industry, starting at age 12. Since opening our doors, we've expanded our network and grown our operations, establishing a firm foothold as the area's foremost roofer.

Many of our team members have served 15–20 years with the company and hold Certified Master Shingler and GAF Certified credentials.
